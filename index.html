Our questionably responsible mission

Some books start conversations. Others start meetings.

The Censored Shelf is a fictional independent bookstore dedicated to books that have been banned, challenged, restricted, removed, or subjected to an unusually dramatic amount of public discussion.

Our goal is to help readers discover challenged literature, learn why certain books have faced objections, and explore the stories and ideas that made people pay attention.

A completely unbiased bookstore*

We encourage readers to learn about challenged books, consider different perspectives, and decide for themselves what belongs on their reading lists.

*We may be slightly biased in favor of reading.
